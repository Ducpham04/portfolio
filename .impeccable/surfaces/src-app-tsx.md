## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a wall of Saigon hand-painted shop signs (biển hiệu tôn sơn tay). A service trade advertises its offer, its price board and its contact in big painted letters, so this site does the same. It refuses the portfolio default of a cream ground, an italic serif, small mono eyebrows, numbered sections and a card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Enamel sheet-metal panels with thick borders and corner bolts. Signal red #d4231c, chrome yellow #ffcc1a, cobalt #1746a6 and sign green #0e7a49 each fill a whole panel, with white enamel and black ink lettering. Painted letters carry a solid offset shade (chữ đổ bóng). Faces: Big Shoulders Display at heavy weights for the lettering, Bungee for plaques, buttons and the price board, Be Vietnam Pro for reading text. Daytime ground is mint lime-wash wall; dark mode is the street at night (near-black asphalt), with the panels unchanged.

STORY: Visitors read who Đức is and what he takes on within one line. They see the projects as hung signs, read the price board, and act through the email/CV/Fiverr buttons painted on the signs.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width red signboard holds the name in huge white letters with a black shade. A yellow band below it reads NHẬN LÀM: WEB · APP · BACKEND SPRING BOOT. A white plaque underneath carries a "LH:" line with two working buttons, Liên hệ (Contact) and Tải CV (Download CV). The header is a narrow black strip with the logo, nav, VI/EN switch and theme toggle. The wall shows at the sign's edges.

FORM: Saigon hand-painted signage wall; position 2 on the ordered list (user-chosen over the assigned candidate); seed:db247612da8b8359.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: project signs hang from two wires and swing about their top edge when hovered or focused (spring, bounded). Section signs settle in once as they enter the viewport. Everything is static under reduced motion.
